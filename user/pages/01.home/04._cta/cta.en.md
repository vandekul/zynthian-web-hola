By assembling your own unit, you get to learn exactly how it works. Don't worry! It is a straightforward process that anyone can complete, and building it yourself offers several distinct advantages:

* **Complete Understanding:** You gain firsthand knowledge of how your device operates.
* **Easy Repairability:** Every single component is available separately, meaning you can easily fix your unit if something goes wrong.
* **Full Customization:** You can tailor the physical unit and its features to perfectly adapt to your specific workflow and needs.
* **Future Upgradeability:** You can upgrade your device while reusing existing parts, which significantly reduces electronic waste and saves you money.

Of course, if you are an experienced maker with the time and technical skills, you can even build your Zynthian completely from scratch. The hardware specifications and schematics are freely available in our [GitHub Repositories](https://github.com/zynthian/zynthian-hw). You can also find step-by-step assembly guides on the [Zynthian Wiki](https://wiki.zynthian.org/index.php/Zynthian_Box_Building?pk_vid=900c6e85f0bf66e41780669219b29b24) and direct technical support from fellow builders on the [Zynthian Forum](https://discourse.zynthian.org/?pk_vid=900c6e85f0bf66e41780669099b29b24).
