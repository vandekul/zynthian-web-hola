You build your own unit and learn how it works. Don't be afraid! It's an easy task that anybody can carry out, and you get a bunch of advantages by doing this way:

* You learn how it works.
* You can repair your unit. Every single part is available separately.
* You can customize your unit, adapting it to your needs.
* You can upgrade your unit, re-using parts, reducing waste and saving money.

Of course, if you are a brave maker and have the time and skills, you can build your zynthian from scratch. The specifications and schematics are freely available in our [github repositories](https://github.com/zynthian/zynthian-hw) and you would find several tutorials in the [wiki](https://wiki.zynthian.org/index.php/Zynthian_Box_Building?pk_vid=900c6e85f0bf66e41780669219b29b24) and guidance in the [zynthian forum](https://discourse.zynthian.org/?pk_vid=900c6e85f0bf66e41780669099b29b24).
