Zynthian is a [community-driven project](https://discourse.zynthian.org?target=_blank)—100% open-source, featuring free software running on open hardware. It is completely configurable and fully hackable! **Free as in Freedom**. You can build your Zynthian from scratch or [buy an official kit](https://shop.zynthian.org).

### Over 100 virtual instruments
Zynthian includes a complete collection of virtual instruments and emulators:
+ **Acoustic and Electric keys** like grand pianos, Rhodes, Wurlitzer, Clavinet, and Farfisa.
+ **Organs** like tonewheel Hammonds, transistor combo organs, and pipe organs.
+ **Iconic Synths** like the DX7, Oberheim, Juno-60, and TB-303.
+ **Virtual Analog Synthesizers** like ZynAddSubFX, Surge-XT, Vaporizer2, NoizeMaker, Odin2, MiMi-d, Monique, Helm, and Amsynth.
+ **Real Hardware Emulators** like the ones developed by [The Usual Suspects](https://theusualsuspects.io/), including Osirus, OsTIrus, Vavra, Xenia, VirtualJV, and JE-8086. (<i>Note: Sourcing a legal copy of the device's firmware ROM file is required for operation</i>).

### Sample-based Instruments
Play massive multisampled sound banks with native support for **SF2**, **SF3**, **SFZ**, **DecentSampler (DS)**, and **GIG** formats. Zynthian comes packed with an extensive built-in collection of sampled instruments, which you can easily expand with extension packages and your own custom libraries.

For the more powerful formats (**SFZ**, **DS**, and **GIG**), instrument size is not limited by memory. The Sfizz and LinuxSampler engines implement advanced play-from-disk algorithms that allow you to load arbitrarily large instruments.

<a class="minilink" href="https://github.com/zynthian/zynthian-packages/tree/main/Soundfonts">more details on sampled instruments &nbsp;&nbsp;&gt;</a>

### Over 1,000 audio effects & MIDI tools
+ **Classic audio effects** featuring essentials like reverb, delay, chorus, distortion, saturation, EQ, compression, flanger, phaser, and wah-wah.
+ **Modern audio effects** including tape simulators, granulators, vocoders, pitch-shifters, and auto-tune.
+ **Neural network modeling** for accurate emulation of non-linear gear like most amplifiers and stompboxes. Install your favorite tones from [Tone3000](https://tone3000.com) with just a few clicks.
+ **Impulse response modeling** for reverb and cabinet emulation.
+ **MIDI tools** like velocity curves, chorders, arpeggiators, monophonic converters, strumming utilities, and harmony analyzers.

### Free extension packages
Zynthian can be easily extended with packages, installable with just one click. The packages can contain sampled instruments, collection of samples, loops, patterns, and IRs. Users can create their own packages and share with the community.

### Production Tools
+ **[Zynseq](/use-case/groovebox-udaw)** is a full-featured step sequencer with live capture, quantization, block operations, and swing. It includes velocity and time humanization, step playback frequency and probability, advanced stutter effects, and parameter automation.
+ **[Clippy](/use-case/groovebox-udaw)** is an audio-clip engine that allows you to play beat-synced audio samples with real-time auto-warping and beat-slicing.
+ **[Launcher](/use-case/groovebox-udaw)** is a performance control grid that lets you trigger patterns, audio clips, or musical phrases arranged in sequential rows. Program precise time signature and tempo changes, phrase loops, and conditional phrase playback to achieve *Da Capo* and *CODA* behavior.
+ **[Zynmixer](/use-case/groovebox-udaw)** is a powerful, flexible mixer that remains intuitive while offering professional channel features: mono/stereo switching, phase inversion, M+S toggles, customizable mixbuses, independent send levels, AB-mixing, and pre-fader listening (PFL).
+ **[SooperLooper](https://sonosaurus.com/sooperlooper/features.html)** by Jesse Chappell is a multi-channel live audio looper inspired by the legendary **Gibson Echoplex Digital Pro (EDP)** hardware. It features immediate loop recording, multi-layer overdubbing, loop multiplication, real-time reversal, and variable feedback decay.
+ **[Native integration with popular MIDI controllers](https://wiki.zynthian.org/index.php/Supported_plug_%26_play_MIDI_controllers)** offers plug-and-play support for industry standards like the Akai APC40, APC Key 25, and MIDI Mix, Novation Launchpads and Launchkeys, the Korg nanoKONTROL, and full Mackie Control Universal (MCU) surfaces.
+ **Multitrack audio recorder** allows you to capture individual instrument and effects chains independently into a pristine multitrack WAV session.
+ **MIDI file recorder/player** lets you easily record, store, and play back standard MIDI files (.MID) on the fly.
+ **[Pure Data](http://www.pd-tutorial.com/english/index.html?target=_blank)** integrates a powerful visual programming language for custom audio and MIDI sound design.
