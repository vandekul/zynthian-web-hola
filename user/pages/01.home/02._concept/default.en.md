Zynthian is a [community-driven project](https://discourse.zynthian.org?target=_blank) and it's 100% open source. Free software on Open hardware. Completely configurable and fully hackable! **Free as in Freedom.** You can build your zynthian from scratch or [buy an official kit](https://shop.zynthian.org).<br>
+ **[over 100 virtual instruments & synths](/engines#synthesizers)**:
 + **accurate emulations of classic instruments:**<br>
pianos, electric pianos (rhodes, wurly, clavinet, ...), hammond organ, combo organ, pipe organ, DX7, oberheim, juno 60, TB-303, ...
 + **virtual analog synthesizers:**<br>
 zynaddsubfx, surge-XT, vaporizer2, noizemaker, odin2, mimi-d, monique, helm, amsynth, synthv1, padthv1, ...
 + **soundfont support:**<br>
 SF2, SF3, SFZ, GIG and DecentSampler formats are supported. Lots of soundfonts are included and many more freely available for installing.
+ **[over 1000 audio & MIDI effects](/engines#effects)**:
 + **[classic audio effects](/engines#effects):** reverb, delay, echo, chorus, distortion, EQ, compressor, wahwah, flanger, phaser, granulator, vocoder, auto-tune...
 + **[neural network modelling](/engines/_engine-list/engine-aidax)** for accurate emulation of non-linear gear like amplifiers, distortion, fuzz, overdrive stomp-boxes, etc. Install your favorite tone from [Tone3000](https://tone3000.com) with just few clicks.
 + **[impulse response modelling](/engines/_engine-list/engine-aidax)** for reverb and cabinet emulation.
 + **midi filters & tools:** map, chorder, arpeggiator, strum, CC-LFO, velocity curves, ...
+ **[step sequencer](/use-case/groovebox-udaw):** a full featured step sequencer with live capture, quantization, block operations, swing, velocity & time humanization, play frequency & chance, advanced stutter, parameter automation ...
+ **[audio clip engine](/use-case/groovebox-udaw):** play beat-synced audio-clips with auto-warping, beat-slicing ... 
+ **[launcher view](/use-case/groovebox-udaw):** can launch patterns, audio clips or phrases (rows, similar to AL scenes). Program time signature & tempo changes, loops and conditional playing (DC + CODA sections), etc.
+ **[powerful mixer](/use-case/groovebox-udaw):** simple by default, but sophisticated when needed: phase & M+S toggles, mixbuses, AB-mixing assignment, pre-fader listening, etc.
+ **[live looping sampler](/use-case/effects-unit):** the [SooperLooper](https://sonosaurus.com/sooperlooper/features.html) engine is capable of immediate loop recording, overdubbing, multiplying, reversing and much more.
+ **[native integration with popular MIDI controllers](https://wiki.zynthian.org/index.php/Supported_plug_%26_play_MIDI_controllers):** Akai APC40, APCKey25, MIDIMix, Novation Launchpads & Launchkeys, Korg nanoKontrol, Mackie (MCU), etc.
+ **multitrack audio recorder:** chains can be recorded independently in a multitrack WAV file.
+ **midi file recorder/player:** standard midi files can be easily recorded and played back.
+ **huge library included:** thousands of presets, soundfonts, samples, audio loops patterns, IRs, etc. freely available as packaged collections that you can install with just one click.
+ **free packages:** an increasing collection of free packages, containing soundfonts, collections of samples, patterns and much more.
+ **[mod-ui](https://wiki.moddevices.com/wiki/MOD_Web_GUI_User_Guide?target=_blank):** a fancy canvas environment for designing crazy arrangements of sound processors.
+ **[pure data](http://www.pd-tutorial.com/english/index.html?target=_blank):** a visual language for sound & midi programming just one-click.
